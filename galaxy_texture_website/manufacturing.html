How Your Umbrella Comes Together

From technical proofing to metal rib assembly, each manufacturing stage follows strict industrial quality gates.

01

Requirement

Consultation on application (promotional giveaway, street retail, or lawn dining), exact canopy sizes, and targeted quantity tiers.

Phase 1 • Spec Scoping
02

Design & Artwork

Digital 2D layout proofing of your logo, brand typography, and pantone colors mapped accurately onto the 8 triangular canopy panels.

Phase 2 • Client Sign-off
03

Material Preparation

Precision geometric cutting of water-resistant oxford/polyester canvas, alongside heat-treating the electroplated metal poles and ribs.

Phase 3 • Frame & Canvas
04

Printing & Assembly

Manual and semi-automatic screen printing on canopy segments followed by rib joint reinforcement stitching and finial fixation.

Phase 4 • Madurai Workshop
05

Dispatch & Delivery

100% open-close tensile stress checks, protective shrink wrapping, heavy-duty carton packing, and direct transport dispatch across India.

Phase 5 • PAN India Transit
